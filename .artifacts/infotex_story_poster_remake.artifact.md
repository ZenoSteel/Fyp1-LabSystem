## 📜 SECTION 1: PROBLEM STATEMENT (Pernyataan Masalah)
### *"The Campus Struggle"*

* **The Blind Walk & Empty Room Mystery:** Students and lecturers walk across multiple building floors only to find locked or unexpectedly occupied computer labs because there is no live room visibility.
* **The Mid-Class Intrusion:** Lecturers booking replacement classes manually often accidentally walk into another lecturer's ongoing class, disrupting teaching sessions due to double-booking conflicts.
* **The Retake Student Nightmare:** Students repeating failed subjects spend days manually cross-referencing paper timetables, frequently ending up with overlapping schedules or missed graduation deadlines.
* **The Forgotten Broken Hardware:** PC screen flickerings or ceiling leaks go unreported or lost in WhatsApp groups, leaving equipment broken for weeks without IT priority.

---

## 📜 SECTION 2: OBJECTIVE (Objektif)
### *"The Mission"*

1. To eliminate physical guesswork by building a mobile app that streams live color-coded occupancy across all **11 JTMK computer labs** directly to phones.
2. To end booking collisions by deploying an AI recommendation engine that auto-detects vacant time gaps and enforces real-time overlap prevention.
3. To solve retake schedule friction by providing a smart algorithm that automatically scans timetables and suggests clash-free class slots.
4. To safeguard campus facilities through an AI-driven maintenance classifier that ranks repairs by urgency (*High/Medium/Low*) for rapid IT action.

---

## 📜 SECTION 3: PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (Penerangan Produk)
### *"The Innovation Story"*

The **AI-Assisted Facility Tracker** was born out of the need to transform static, paper-based lab management into a proactive, intelligent campus ecosystem. Built on **Android Studio (Java)** and backed by **Google Firebase**, the application bridges the communication gap between Students, Lecturers, and IT Admins with zero data latency:

* **The Core Breakthrough (>50% AI Integration):** Instead of making users search manually, the app thinks ahead. It features **1-Tap AI Free Gap Auto-Fill**, a **Natural Language Processing (NLP) Chatbot** for direct commands (*"book dcn Monday 1pm 4pm"*), an **AI Retake Assistant**, and automated **Facility Issue Prioritization**.
* **Real-Time Synchronized Backbone:** Powered by **Firebase Realtime Database** and **Cloud Firestore**, every booking, status flip (*AVAILABLE vs UNDER MAINTENANCE*), or cancellation updates instantly across all campus devices.

## 📜 SECTION 4: APPLICATION (Aplikasi)
### *"How the Story Plays Out for Users"*

* 🎓 **For Students (Empowerment):** Check live room status from anywhere, find lecturer office availability, report broken PC equipment instantly, and find clash-free retake classes in 1 tap.
* 👨‍🏫 **For Lecturers (Speed & Certainty):** Type a natural sentence to the AI Assistant to reserve a room, view live occupancy logs, and get instant AI suggestions for conflict-free replacement slots.
* ⚙️ For IT Admins (Control & Intelligence):** View system health metrics, override lab access switches during exams, and receive an AI-prioritized feed that puts critical hazards (*like server room leaks*) at the top.

## 📜 SECTION 5: IMPACT (Impak)
### *"The Transformation"*

* **Zero Booking Overlaps:** Eradicates mid-class interruptions and double-booked labs across JTMK.
* **Booking in Seconds, Not Minutes:** Lecturers save significant administrative time using 1-Tap Auto-Fill instead of filling tedious paper forms.
* **Faster Graduation Routes:** Enables retake students to secure required subjects without delay, improving faculty graduation rates.
* **Proactive Campus Preservation:** Emergency issues (*High Priority*) receive immediate technical attention before causing costly hardware damage.
