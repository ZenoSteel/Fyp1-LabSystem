# PRIeX / InfoTeX Poster Text (Short & Concise "Story of Why" Edition)

## 📜 SECTION 1: PROBLEM STATEMENT (Pernyataan Masalah)
### *"The Campus Struggle"*

1. **The Blind Walk:** Students and lecturers walk across floors only to find locked or unexpectedly occupied computer labs due to zero live visibility.
2. **The Mid-Class Intrusion:** Manual booking leads to double-booking clashes, causing replacement classes to accidentally interrupt ongoing lectures.
3. **The Retake Nightmare:** Repeating students waste days cross-referencing paper timetables, risking schedule overlaps and graduation delays.
4. **Unreported Broken Hardware:** Equipment damages like flickering monitors or ceiling leaks get lost in WhatsApp chats, remaining unrepaired for weeks.

---

## 📜 SECTION 2: OBJECTIVE (Objektif)
### *"The Mission"*

1. To stream live color-coded occupancy across all **11 JTMK computer labs** directly to mobile phones.
2. To end booking collisions via an AI recommendation engine that detects free time gaps and enforces overlap prevention.
3. To eliminate retake scheduling friction with an AI assistant that suggests clash-free course slots automatically.
4. To safeguard campus infrastructure using an AI classifier that prioritizes repairs by urgency (*High/Medium/Low*).

---

## 📜 SECTION 3: PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (Penerangan Produk)
### *"The Innovation Story"*

The **AI-Assisted Facility Tracker** transforms static paper lab management into a proactive smart campus ecosystem. Built on **Android Studio (Java)** and **Google Firebase**, it connects Students, Lecturers, and IT Admins with zero latency:

* **>50% AI Core:** Features 1-Tap Free Gap Auto-Fill, NLP Chatbot for direct commands (*"book dcn Monday 1pm 4pm"*), AI Retake Assistant, and automated Issue Prioritization.
* **Real-Time Backbone:** Powered by **Firebase Realtime DB & Cloud Firestore** for millisecond-level status updates across all campus devices.

---

## 📜 SECTION 4: APPLICATION (Aplikasi)
### *"How the Story Plays Out"*

* 🎓 **For Students:** Check room availability from anywhere, find lecturer office locations, report hardware issues, and enroll in retake classes in 1 tap.
* 👨‍🏫 **For Lecturers:** Reserve lab slots instantly via natural NLP Chatbot commands and receive automatic AI suggestions for conflict-free replacement classes.
* ⚙️ **For IT Admins:** Monitor daily usage metrics, override lab access switches during exams, and receive an AI-prioritized feed for urgent repairs.

---

## 📜 SECTION 5: IMPACT (Impak)
### *"The Transformation"*

1. **Zero Booking Overlaps:** Completely eradicates mid-class interruptions and double-booked labs across JTMK.
2. **Booking in Seconds:** 1-Tap AI Auto-Fill reduces lecturer booking time from minutes to seconds.
3. **Faster Graduation Routes:** Helps retake students secure required subjects without delay, improving faculty graduation rates.
4. **Proactive Campus Preservation:** Emergency issues (*High Priority*) receive immediate technical response before costly damage occurs.
