# InfoTeX Exhibition Poster Content (English Edition)
**Project Title:** AI-Assisted Facility Tracker and Reservation System (Fyp1)

---

## 1. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
The **AI-Assisted Facility Tracker and Reservation System** is a mobile innovation built on **Android Studio (Java)** and **Google Firebase**. The system integrates **11 smart/AI modules** to transform static lab management into a role-based smart ecosystem (*Student, Lecturer, Admin*).

* **>50% AI Integration:** Features 1-Tap Free Gap Auto-Fill, Natural Language Processing (NLP) Chatbot, AI Retake Assistant, and automated facility issue prioritization.
* **Distributed Architecture:** Real-time lab listing powered by **Firebase Realtime Database** for millisecond-level status updates with **zero latency**.

---

## 2. APPLICATION (Multi-Role Access)
1. **Student:** Check real-time lab availability, utilize the AI Retake Assistant, search for lecturer office locations, and report facility damages.
2. **Lecturer:** Reserve lab rooms using the single-message NLP Chatbot, view active reservations, and receive automatic AI schedule recommendations.
3. **Admin:** Monitor daily usage metrics, manage the Master Timetable (*Add/Delete sessions*), and review AI-prioritized facility issue feeds (*High/Medium/Low*).

---

## 3. PROBLEM STATEMENT
1. **Lack of Real-Time Visibility:** Students and lecturers struggle to check the real-time availability of **11 JTMK computer labs** instantly.
2. **Timetable & Reservation Clashing:** Ad-hoc manual booking frequently causes double-booking conflicts that interrupt official classes.
3. **Difficulty for Retake Students:** Students retaking failed subjects face severe challenges finding clash-free lab slots across parallel sections.
4. **Unorganized Facility Reporting:** Equipment damages and lab issues are reported informally without priority classification.

---

## 4. OBJECTIVES
1. To develop an interactive mobile application displaying real-time availability for **11 JTMK computer labs**.
2. To integrate an AI recommendation engine that detects continuous free time windows and prevents overlap conflicts.
3. To provide an AI Retake Assistant that helps students find clash-free schedules for retake courses.
4. To provide an Admin Command Center featuring AI issue prioritization (*High/Medium/Low*) for proactive maintenance.

---

## 5. IMPACT
1. **Enhanced Lab Management Efficiency:** Eliminates reservation clashes and optimizes room occupancy rates.
2. **Time Saving:** The 1-Tap Auto-Fill feature reduces lecturer booking time from minutes to seconds.
3. **Streamlined Academic Workflow:** Enables retake students to find and enroll in clash-free course sections rapidly.
4. **Proactive Facility Maintenance:** High-priority issue reports (*e.g., water leaks or hardware failures*) allow immediate administrative intervention.
